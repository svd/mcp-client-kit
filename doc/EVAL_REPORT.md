## Overall verdict

Twenty-one of 23 servers pass outright; github and notion are partial only because a handful of
tools hit live 403/404/entitlement walls and were marked `_probe_status: "inconclusive"` instead of
being typed from an error envelope. No wrapper needed a hand fix, and the skill's defining trait is
restraint — sqlite `read_query`, github's method-multiplexed readers, filesystem `directory_tree`,
aws-knowledge's dynamic product catalogs, stackoverflow's response-keyed `Data`, and linear's
not-found envelopes were all left `Any`/`dict` rather than force-typed.

## Mode coverage gaps

Thirteen servers reached Mode B or C; ten landed Mode A only. For eight of the ten (cloudflare-docs,
codegraph, deepwiki, exa, fetch, git, huggingface, openzeppelin-solidity, playwright) Mode A is
honest: prose, CLI stdout, Markdown reports, fenced Solidity — with `json.loads()` actually
attempted and failing. Two are gaps richer probing could close:

- **everything** — 12 of 13 tools read as `str` largely because probes see only text parts;
  `image`/`resource`/`resource_link` blocks are invisible, the same blind spot that pinned
  playwright's `browser_take_screenshot` at `-> Any`.
- **context7 `resolve-library-id`** — record-shaped data shipped as a delimited plaintext listing:
  parseable, never parsed.

Mode D is 0/23. Across roughly 80 advisory candidates (linear 46, notion 13, playwright 10,
openzeppelin 7), every one was an identity or input-only argument, or structurally incompatible —
github's `method` switches between dict- and list-shaped responses, and notion's and
stackoverflow's real polymorphism lives in the *response* key, which codegen overloads cannot see.
aws-knowledge shows the workaround that does work: multi-probe plus deep-merge into one `total=False`
union.

## Known issues

1. **Empty or degraded state erases shape** — linear's near-empty workspace (~20 tools unobservable),
   notion's empty lists, memory's per-invocation fresh graph, semgrep's "No findings found" and four
   decommissioned tools, playwright's three precondition failures.
2. **Depth stops at one level** — time's `source`/`target`, semgrep's `authDetails`, memory's
   `entities`/`relations`, stackoverflow's `Errors` all stay bare containers.
3. **Unpinned launch commands drift** — sqlite crashed on an `mcp` SDK bump until pinned to `mcp<2`;
   huggingface silently lost three tools since the prior run.

## Next steps

1. **Seed or borrow real state before probing** (linear, notion, memory, semgrep, playwright) — the
   single largest source of unobservable shapes.
2. **Probe the full content-block union**, not just text parts — the likeliest lift from A to B/C.
3. **Re-run on one pinned engine**: the matrix currently mixes 0.8.0.dev1, 0.8.0, and 0.9.0.dev1
   (flagged at `eval_harness/report.py:293`), so no result is attributable to a single release.
